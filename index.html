ABOUT AGROLINK

Connecting Nigerian Farmers
Directly to Buyers

A direct-to-market agricultural trading platform built to eliminate middlemen, ensure fair prices for farmers, and deliver fresh produce directly to buyers across Nigeria.

Our Mission

To empower Nigerian farmers by giving them direct access to buyers, fair market prices, and secure payment systems.

Our Vision

A Nigeria where every farmer has access to a fair, transparent marketplace and every buyer can trace food directly to the farm.

How It Works
Simple, transparent, and secure — from farm to doorstep
1
Farmer Lists
Farmer creates an account and lists fresh produce with price, quantity and location.
2
Buyer Orders
Buyer browses the marketplace, finds what they need, and places a secure order.
3
Escrow Payment
Payment held securely by Paystack until buyer confirms delivery.
4
Farmer Paid
After delivery confirmed, funds released directly to farmer's account.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

AgroLink ("we", "us", "our") operates a platform that connects farmers directly with buyers across Nigeria. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using AgroLink, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: name, email address, phone number, password (stored hashed), and account role (farmer, buyer, or admin).

Farm & listing information: farm location/state, product listings, prices, quantities, and product photos, if you register as a farmer.

Verification (KYC) documents: identity or farm-ownership documents you upload for the farmer verification process.

Order & payment information: order history, delivery details, and transaction records. Card and bank details are collected and processed directly by our payment processor, Paystack — AgroLink does not store your full card number.

Communications: messages sent through in-app chat between farmers and buyers, and any messages you send us via the contact form.

Usage & device data: basic technical data such as browser type and error logs, used to keep the platform running and to fix bugs.

2. How We Use Your Information

• To create and manage your account and verify farmer identities

• To process orders, payments, and escrow releases

• To enable communication between farmers and buyers

• To send order updates, OTP codes, and important account notices

• To detect fraud, abuse, and maintain platform security

• To improve the platform and fix technical issues

3. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

• Paystack, our payment processor, to process payments and escrow

• Supabase, our backend/database provider, which stores account and order data on our behalf

• The other party to a transaction (e.g. a buyer sees a farmer's public listing and business name; a farmer sees a buyer's delivery details for an order)

• Law enforcement or regulators, where required by law

4. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to comply with legal, tax, and dispute-resolution obligations. You may request deletion of your account as described in Section 6 below, subject to records we are legally required to retain.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures — including encrypted connections (HTTPS), password hashing, and role-based access controls — to protect your data. No system is 100% secure, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password.

6. Your Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023, you have the right to:

• Access the personal data we hold about you

• Request correction of inaccurate data

• Request deletion of your account and associated data

• Withdraw consent for optional processing (e.g. marketing messages)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 9.

7. Cookies & Local Storage

AgroLink uses browser local/session storage to keep you signed in, remember your cart, and remember display preferences like dark mode. These are not used for third-party advertising tracking.

8. Children's Privacy

AgroLink is not intended for use by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of AgroLink after changes means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at taiwoolami00@gmail.com.

Terms of Service

Last updated: 24 July 2026

These Terms of Service ("Terms") govern your use of AgroLink, a platform connecting farmers and buyers across Nigeria. By registering for or using AgroLink, you agree to these Terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the platform.

1. Eligibility & Accounts

You must be at least 18 years old and able to form a binding contract under Nigerian law to use AgroLink. You agree to provide accurate, current information when registering, and to keep your login credentials confidential. You are responsible for all activity on your account.

AgroLink offers two primary account roles — Farmer (listing and selling produce) and Buyer (browsing and purchasing produce). Farmer accounts may be subject to a verification process before certain features are unlocked.

2. Marketplace Conduct

• Farmers must list only produce/goods they genuinely have available, with accurate pricing, quantity, and description.

• Buyers must provide accurate delivery information and pay for orders they place in good faith.

• Users must not use AgroLink for any unlawful purpose, to defraud another user, or to circumvent the platform's payment/escrow system for a transaction initiated on AgroLink.

• Harassment, hate speech, or abusive behaviour toward other users through the messaging system is prohibited and may result in suspension.

3. Orders, Payments & Escrow

When a buyer places an order, payment is collected up front and held in escrow via our payment processor, Paystack, rather than paid directly to the farmer.

Funds are released to the farmer once the buyer confirms delivery, or automatically after the applicable confirmation window, whichever these Terms or the platform's stated process provides for.

AgroLink is a facilitator of the transaction between farmer and buyer, not the seller of the produce itself. We are not responsible for the quality, safety, or legality of items listed by farmers, though we may assist in resolving disputes as described below.

4. Cancellations & Disputes

Orders may be cancelled prior to dispatch in accordance with the options presented in your order dashboard. Once a buyer confirms delivery, funds are released and the order is considered complete.

If there is a problem with an order (e.g. produce not delivered, significantly not as described), contact us at taiwoolami00@gmail.com with your order details before confirming delivery so we can help resolve the issue.

5. Fees

AgroLink is free to join. Any transaction or service fees, if introduced, will be clearly disclosed before you complete an order or withdrawal.

6. Withdrawals (Farmers)

Farmers may request withdrawal of released funds to a linked bank account. Withdrawal requests are reviewed and processed as shown in the Farmer Dashboard and may take a reasonable processing period to complete.

7. Reviews & Content

Buyers may leave ratings and reviews for farmers after a completed order. Reviews must be honest and based on genuine transactions. AgroLink may remove reviews or content that are fraudulent, abusive, or violate these Terms.

8. Account Suspension & Termination

We may suspend or terminate accounts that violate these Terms, engage in fraud, or misuse the platform. You may stop using AgroLink and request account deletion at any time by contacting us.

9. Limitation of Liability

AgroLink is provided "as is." To the maximum extent permitted by law, AgroLink is not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from your use of the platform, including disputes between farmers and buyers, except where such liability cannot be excluded under Nigerian law.

10. Governing Law

These Terms are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Nigerian courts.

11. Changes to These Terms

We may update these Terms from time to time. Continued use of AgroLink after changes take effect means you accept the revised Terms.

12. Contact Us
